Mokichi Okada (Meishu-sama)
HIS LIFE

A Life Dedicated to Beauty & Humanity

Mokichi Okada was born in Tokyo in 1882. Throughout his life he developed a spiritual philosophy centered on compassion, gratitude, beauty, nature and the wellbeing of humanity.

His followers refer to him as Meishu-sama, meaning "Lord of Light."

1882

Birth

He continued developing and teaching Jyorei.

Development of Zuiunkyō became part of his vision for a beautiful spiritual environment.

1955

Passing

His physical life ended in Atami at age 72.

SPIRITUAL PRACTICE

Jyorei

Jyorei is a spiritual practice associated with Meishu-sama in which one person offers the practice toward another with an emphasis on compassion, tranquility and spiritual purification.

Shumei describes Jyorei as "purification of the spirit" and welcomes people who wish to experience it at its centers.

Jyorei is presented here as a spiritual and religious practice. Claims about healing should not be understood as established medical treatment or a replacement for professional medical care.
